## 1. Payments: instant payments, VoP, ISO 20022, Request-to-Pay, VRP, pay-by-bank, cross-border, stablecoins, digital euro, tokenised deposits

### Takeaway
Instant payments plus Verification of Payee are now **mandatory table stakes in the euro area (since 9 Oct 2025)**, and the UK has a live commercial-VRP scheme (UKPI Wave 1, 2 June 2026). The genuinely new frontier for 2026-27 is (a) agent-initiated and bank-to-bank "pay by bank" at scale, (b) regulated stablecoins (GENIUS Act in force, rules still being finalised) and tokenised deposits (US big-bank network targeted early 2027; UK programmable-sterling pilot through end-2026), and (c) the digital euro, which is in trilogue with first issuance not before 2029.

### Cited Findings

**Instant payments and Verification of Payee (EU)**
- Euro-area PSPs had to offer Verification of Payee (VoP) from **9 October 2025** under the Instant Payments Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/886); the ECB lists 9 Oct 2025 as the VoP date for euro-area Member States and an extended deadline of **9 July 2027** for non-euro Member States — [ECB Instant Payments Regulation page](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/retail/instant_payments/html/instant_payments_regulation.ht.html); [Mondaq/Germany on VoP under IPR](https://www.mondaq.com/germany/financial-services/1669532/verification-of-payee-requirements-vop-under-the-eus-instant-payments-regulation-ipr). (Qonto's developer docs give 9 Jan 2027 for non-euro PSPs, which conflicts — [Qonto docs](https://docs.qonto.com/api-reference/business-api/payments-transfers/sepa-transfers/introduction).)
- The EPC's dedicated VoP Scheme Rulebook entered into force on **5 October 2025**, days before the regulatory deadline; VoP applies to both standard and instant credit transfers, and to EMIs and PIs as well as banks — [Finextra long read on IPR and 9 Oct deadline](https://www.finextra.com/the-long-read/1471/europes-instant-payments-regulation-and-the-9-october-deadline-explained); [RedCompass Labs 2025 SEPA instant deadlines](https://www.redcompasslabs.com/insights/2025-sepa-instant-payments-deadlines).

**US instant payments (FedNow / RTP)**
- RTP leads FedNow in both transaction count and value; RTP handled ~128 million transactions worth ~$480 billion in Q1 2026, while FedNow's average transaction is ~$99,414 — [Richmond Fed Economic Brief 26-28](https://www.richmondfed.org/publications/research/economic_brief/2026/eb_26-28).
- FedNow processed 2.7 million transactions valued at $271.3 billion in Q1 2026 and, at its third anniversary (July 2026), had more than 1,800 banks and credit unions on the network — [Digital Transactions, Feb 2026](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/?p=1334073); [Digital Transactions, FedNow third anniversary](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/fednow-marks-a-third-anniversary-tally-at-more-than-1800-banks-and-credit-unions/).
- Monthly RTP value grew from $28B (Jan 2025) to $195B (Apr 2026); daily RTP volume now exceeds 2 million transactions — [Pulse: RTP hits another mark](https://www.pulse.bot/banking/news/rtp-hits-another-banner-real-time-payment-mark-b6ab3bdb-6b07-4082-a3c5-9bb7425c79eb/). (Figures across sources are not directly reconcilable; treat exact counts as indicative.)

**ISO 20022**
- The Swift CBPR+ coexistence period for cross-border **payment instructions ended 22 November 2025**; non-instruction (reporting/statement) messages have an extended coexistence with end planned for **SR 2028**; MT-to-ISO contingency conversion continues with charges; from **November 2026** unstructured-only addresses will no longer be accepted for most CBPR+ payments — [Swift CBPR+ roadmap page](https://www.swift.com/iso-20022-financial-institutions); [Swift CBPR+ phase 2 roadmap v4.2 (PDF)](https://www.swift.com/sites/default/files/files/cbpr-phase-2-roadmap_detailed_v4-2.pdf); [Mambu: CBPR+ is live](https://mambu.com/en/insights/articles/cbpr-is-live-what-iso-20022-means-in-practice).

**Request-to-Pay (SEPA RTP)**
- The EPC published SRTP Inter-Service-Provider API specs in July 2025 (APIs mandated for reachability since Nov 2023) and Implementation Guidelines v4.0 in June 2025; in March 2026 the EPC issued guidelines on accelerating SRTP adoption (interoperability, reachability, governance) — [Vixio: EPC finalises SRTP API specs](https://www.vixio.com/insights/pc-epc-finalises-api-specifications-sepa-request-pay-interoperability); [EPC SRTP Implementation Guidelines v4.0](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/node/27161); [Lexis EU FS daily round-up 19 Mar 2026](https://app-prod-legal.azurewebsites.net/news/eu-financial-services-daily-round-up-19-march-2026).

### Inferences
- For an EU-licensed bank, SCT Inst send/receive 24/7, VoP on every outbound transfer, and ISO 20022-native (structured-address) data models are non-negotiable baseline scope; a greenfield bank should design its ledger around ISO 20022 structured data rather than retrofitting.
- VRP/cVRP (UK) and SRTP (EU) are the same product idea from two directions: user-controlled, revocable, variable-amount mandates. A bank that exposes mandate controls (caps, expiry, per-merchant limits) in-app is "differentiating" in 2026 and will likely be table-stakes once UKPI Wave 2 and PSD3/PSR land.
- Tokenised deposits are an institutional story in 2026; retail-facing programmable money (escrow-like conditional P2P payments, mortgage-completion atomic settlement) is **speculative** until the UK pilot reports. Stablecoin on/off-ramps and holding are **differentiating** for a neobank now (Klarna, Revolut, KakaoBank all moving), with the regulatory window (GENIUS by Jan 2027; MiCA already in force) making it buildable.
- US pay-by-bank is still a small share (1.5%) and awareness-limited; the opportunity is in UX (one-tap bank checkout with instant settlement) rather than rails.

## 2. Open finance: PSD3/PSR, FiDA, UK Open Banking/Smart Data, Israel, Brazil, US CFPB 1033

### Takeaway
PSD3/PSR have a political agreement (27 Nov 2025) and final compromise texts (23 Apr 2026) but as of these sources were awaiting OJ publication; FiDA was still in trilogue with no confirmed deal; the UK published an FCA Open Finance roadmap (April 2026) and is operating cVRP; Israel adopted the Berlin Group standard but deferred payment-company obligations; the US CFPB 1033 rule is enjoined and being rewritten, so US open banking is proceeding via private bilateral deals.

### Cited Findings

**PSD3 / PSR (EU)**
- Parliament and Council reached provisional political agreement on PSD3/PSR on **27 November 2025**; Council published final compromise texts on **23 April 2026**; ECON approved the text on **5 May 2026**; OJ publication expected H2 2026 — [Mondaq: PSD3/PSR final compromise texts published](https://www.mondaq.com/ireland/financial-services/1780752/psd3psr-final-compromise-texts-are-published); [William Fry](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/psd3-psr-final-compromise-texts-are-published/); [Worldline: scope and timeline locked for PSD3/PSR](https://worldline.com/en/home/main-navigation/resources/blogs/2026/the-scope-and-timeline-are-locked-in-for-psd3-and-psr-what-should-psps-know); [Council doc ST-8220-2026](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-8220-2026-INIT/en/pdf).
- The compromise brings tighter harmonisation, crypto-asset payment rules and stronger consumer transparency; Council proposals include a consent-management "panel" (dashboard) and fraud provisions — [Mondaq: EU reaches compromise on PSD3/PSR](https://www.mondaq.com/financial-services/1781162/eu-reaches-compromise-on-psd3-and-psr-tighter-harmonisation-crypto-rules-and-stronger-consumer-transparency); [Capco: PSD3/PSR consent panel and fraud](https://www.capco.com/intelligence/capco-intelligence/psd3-psr-the-council-of-the-eu-proposal-on-the-consent-panel-and-fraud).

**FiDA (EU)**
- FiDA trilogues started April 2025; a 2026 Council working document says the Council "has yet to reach a sufficient degree of convergence" on treatment of gatekeepers; as of May 2026 there were "signs of renewed movement after months of silence"; vendor tracker expected adoption around mid-2026 with phased application from 2027 — [Council WK-5041-2026](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/WK-5041-2026-INIT/en/pdf); [Projective: FiDA back in motion (22 May 2026)](https://www.projectivegroup.com/fida-back-in-motion-from-ambitious-to-workable/); [Fiskil FiDA tracker](https://www.fiskil.com/open-finance-tracker/standard/fida); [PayTechLaw: what's next for FiDA](https://paytechlaw.com/en/whats-next-for-fida/). **UK Open Banking / Smart Data / Open Finance**

**US CFPB Section 1033**
- Rule finalised Oct 2024; a Kentucky federal court enjoined enforcement; the April 2026 compliance deadline passed without effect; in Jan 2026 CFPB shifted to full rulemaking for a rewrite (open question: whether data providers may charge fees); banks pursue private bilateral data-access deals meanwhile — [American Banker: on the day of a would-be deadline](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/on-the-day-of-a-would-be-deadline-open-banking-is-in-flux); [CU Times: CFPB shifts to full rulemaking (29 Jan 2026)](https://www.cutimes.com/amp/2026/01/29/cfpb-shifts-to-full-rulemaking-for-section-1033-rewrite-after-credit-union-push/); [Vixio: CFPB decision to vacate 1033 creates confusion](https://www.vixio.com/insights/pc-cfpbs-decision-vacate-1033-rule-creates-confusion-us-open-banking); [Open Banking Tracker 1033 status](https://openbankingtracker.com/guides/section-1033-status).

### Inferences
- A greenfield EU bank should build to the PSD3/PSR compromise text now (consent dashboard, dedicated API with no obstacles, shared fraud liability), and design its data model so FiDA-style broader data (savings, loans, insurance, investments) can be exposed later.
- For Israel, implementing the Berlin Group NextGenPSD2 standard (as adopted by BoI Directive 368) is the practical path and gives EU interoperability for free.
- US open banking will remain aggregator/bilateral-driven through 2027; building on FDX-style APIs and an aggregator (Plaid/MX/Akoya) remains necessary.

### Inferences
- "AI chat support" is table stakes; **agent that executes** (open a pot, move money, set a rule, block a merchant) with explicit scoped consent is differentiating in 2026 and will be expected by 2027. - Credit-scoring AI must be built to AI Act high-risk requirements (data governance, logging, human oversight, explanations) with a hard deadline of 2 Dec 2027; explainability (reason codes) is also a GDPR Art. 22 expectation today.

## 4. Identity: EUDI Wallet, verifiable credentials, reusable KYC, passkeys

### Takeaway
Banks, PSPs and e-money issuers in the EU must accept the EU Digital Identity Wallet for strong customer authentication by **24 December 2027** (eIDAS 2.0, Reg. (EU) 2024/1183, Art. 5f); the wallet's payment-SCA profile is TS12. Passkeys are already deployed at scale by fintechs and some banks but sit awkwardly with the prescriptive PSD2 SCA RTS; PSD3/PSR is expected to be more outcome-based.

### Cited Findings
- Deadline: banks/payment institutions/e-money issuers must accept the EUDI Wallet for SCA by **24 December 2027** under eIDAS 2.0 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, Article 5f); acceptance, not migration, is required — [Fintech Global: what banks need to know before the EUDI deadline (Sep 2026)](https://fintech.global/2026/09/04/what-banks-need-to-know-before-the-eudi-wallet-deadline/); [walt.id: eIDAS2 financial services](https://walt.id/eidas2/financial-services); [OneSpan: why European banks must act now](https://www.onespan.com/node/135596). (All passkey sources are vendor-adjacent.)

### Inferences
- Design authentication around FIDO2/passkeys as the default factor now (table stakes for a neobank), with an OpenID4VP/OpenID4VCI verifier so the EUDI wallet can be accepted for SCA and onboarding well before Dec 2027 (differentiating in 2026-27).
- "Reusable KYC" via verifiable credentials (PID + bank-issued attestation) is the realistic route to sub-minute onboarding and to cross-border onboarding (EU + Israel), but depends on member-state wallet rollouts (due by end-2026 under eIDAS 2.0 — not verified in this pass).

### Inferences (unsolved pain points = disruption opportunities)
- **Multi-bank fragmentation**: customers hold money in several places (J.D. Power "soft switching"); nobody in the sources offers a bank that *orchestrates* the other accounts (aggregate + auto-sweep + act). Open finance APIs + agent make this buildable.
- **Rate/fee opacity and inertia**: McKinsey's agent-erosion thesis implies customers want automatic best-rate behaviour; a bank that does this *for* the customer (transparent, published fee/rate logic) converts a threat into a feature.
- **Problem resolution and fraud handling**: fraud reimbursement is now regulated in the UK but inconsistent; trust is the top switching driver. Real-time, explainable fraud decisions with in-app dispute status and guaranteed timelines remain an open gap.
- **Transactional relationship**: 79% see banking as commoditised; agentic, proactive advice (Starling/Revolut) is the current attempt; results are not yet measured in the sources.
- **Onboarding**: minutes, not seconds; reusable KYC via EUDI/verifiable credentials is the route to sub-minute onboarding.
